SMS Notifications Opt-In

Sentinels Kiosk sends transactional text messages on behalf of organizations that use our visitor-management platform — for example, alerting a host that their visitor has arrived, or notifying an administrator about a security or offline event. We never send marketing or promotional texts. If your organization uses Sentinels Kiosk, use this page to opt in to SMS notifications.

Opt in to text messages

Enter your mobile number and check the consent box. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP.
